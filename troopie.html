Built For Australia

The Adventure Club Australia Troopie is more than just a touring vehicle. It was built with one goal in mind — creating the ultimate self-sufficient adventure platform capable of exploring Australia's most remote destinations.

Starting life as a 2023 Toyota LandCruiser VDJ78, this vehicle has been transformed through extensive modifications into one of the most capable touring vehicles in the country.

With 405 rear wheel horsepower, premium suspension upgrades, solar charging, battery management and a complete Headspace conversion, this Troopie is equally at home on the beach, in the desert or deep in the Australian bush.
