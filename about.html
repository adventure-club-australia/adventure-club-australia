Why Adventure Club Australia Exists

Adventure Club Australia was created to make unique travel vehicles more accessible, affordable and community-driven.

Most adventure vehicles cost a fortune to build, insure, store and maintain — yet spend much of their lives parked. At the same time, many Australians dream of escaping more often but can't justify owning multiple purpose-built vehicles.

Adventure Club Australia bridges that gap by creating a shared fleet of iconic adventure vehicles that members can enjoy without the full financial commitment of ownership.

Our Mission

Own the Adventure. Share the Journey.

We believe adventure should be accessible to more Australians. Through memberships and vehicle sharing, we aim to grow a fleet that delivers unforgettable experiences while building a passionate community of explorers.
