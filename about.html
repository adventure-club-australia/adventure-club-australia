Building Australia's Shared Adventure Fleet.

Adventure Club Australia was created to make incredible travel experiences more accessible while building a community around freedom, exploration and adventure.

Why We Exist

Adventure Club Australia was born from a simple observation.

Most adventure vehicles cost tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars to build, insure, maintain and store.


Despite that investment, many of these vehicles spend most of their lives parked in sheds and garages waiting for the next trip.


At the same time, thousands of Australians dream of taking more road trips, camping adventures and off-grid escapes, but can't justify owning multiple purpose-built vehicles.


Adventure Club Australia exists to bridge that gap.


Our vision is to create a community-powered fleet that gives members access to incredible adventure vehicles while sharing the costs, experiences and journey together.

Meet The Founder

Hi, I'm Jake.


I've always had a passion for building unique vehicles and exploring Australia.


Over the years I've built a collection of vehicles that each offer a completely different adventure experience — from a retro Volkswagen Beetle camper to a 405 horsepower Toyota Troopie built for remote off-grid touring.


The idea behind Adventure Club Australia came from wanting to create something bigger than a collection of vehicles.


I wanted to create a community where people could access incredible adventures, share experiences and help build something unique together.


Adventure Club Australia isn't just about vehicles.


It's about freedom, exploration, community and creating memories that last a lifetime.
