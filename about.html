Our Story

More Than Vehicles. More Than Holidays. More Than A Club.

Adventure Club Australia exists to help ordinary Australians experience extraordinary adventures through unique vehicles and experiences they may never have the opportunity to own themselves.

Why Adventure Club Australia Exists


Adventure Club Australia was born from a passion for building unique vehicles and exploring Australia.


Over time, it became impossible to ignore one simple fact:


Incredible adventure vehicles spend most of their lives parked while countless Australians dream of experiencing them.


Many people love the idea of owning a custom touring vehicle, retro camper or off-grid adventure rig, but the reality is that ownership can be expensive and out of reach.


Adventure Club Australia was created to bridge that gap.


Instead of owning the dream, members gain access to it.

What We Believe

Everything we do is guided by four simple principles.

Adventure

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to create unforgettable memories.

Community

The best adventures are shared with like-minded people.

Access

Extraordinary experiences should be available to more Australians.

Growth

Every new vehicle and experience should create more opportunities for members.

The Vision

Adventure Club Australia is building a future where adventure is more accessible.

Today our fleet includes vehicles like Bedbug and the Troopie.


Tomorrow it may include custom campers, boats, tiny homes, off-grid cabins and entirely new adventure experiences.


Every new addition creates more opportunities for members to explore, connect and experience something extraordinary.


This is not about building a fleet.


It's about building Australia's most exciting adventure community.

Our Mission

Own The Adventure. Share The Journey.


Helping ordinary Australians experience extraordinary adventures through unique vehicles and experiences they may never have the opportunity to own themselves.
