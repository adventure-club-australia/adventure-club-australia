The dream retro escape.

Meet Bedbug — a one-of-a-kind 1968 Volkswagen Beetle transformed into the ultimate retro getaway vehicle.

Combining timeless vintage styling with modern engineering, Bedbug has been rebuilt to deliver a unique travel experience without sacrificing comfort or reliability. Underneath the iconic Beetle body sits a modern 2.0L Subaru motor, providing dependable performance, improved fuel efficiency and confidence for coastal cruising.

Upgraded suspension and braking systems create a smoother, safer and more comfortable drive, while the custom teardrop camper gives you everything needed for unforgettable weekend escapes.

Built for slow mornings and coastal getaways.

Designed for couples seeking adventure at a relaxed pace, Bedbug is perfect for beachside mornings, hinterland road trips, caravan parks, sunset coffee stops and weekend escapes close to home.
