Meet The Founder

Hi, I'm Jake.


I've always had a passion for building unique vehicles and exploring Australia.

Over the years I've built a collection of adventure vehicles that each offer a completely different experience — from a retro Volkswagen Beetle camper to a 405 horsepower off-grid Toyota Troopie.

Like many Australians, I realised these vehicles spend most of their lives parked despite costing hundreds of thousands of dollars to build and maintain.

That's where the idea for Adventure Club Australia was born.

The vision is simple: Create Australia's first community-powered adventure fleet, giving everyday Australians access to incredible travel vehicles without the cost and commitment of ownership.
