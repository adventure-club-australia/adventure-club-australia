Our Vision

Adventure should feel possible.

Most adventure vehicles cost tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars and spend most of their life parked. Adventure Club Australia was created to make incredible getaways more accessible, while building a fleet that members can help shape and grow.

Shared Access

Members get access to a growing range of unique vehicles built for different styles of adventure.

Community Driven

Every member becomes part of the story, helping build a club around travel, freedom and exploration.

Fleet Growth

Memberships help fund future builds, new vehicles and better experiences for the entire club.

Current Fleet

Two very different ways to escape.

From relaxed retro coastal weekends to serious off-grid touring, the first Adventure Club vehicles are built around experience, character and freedom.

Retro Coastal Escape

Bedbug

A one-of-a-kind retro VW Beetle camper designed for small local trips, sealed-road adventures, caravan parks, coffee mornings and relaxed weekend getaways.

Ultimate Expedition

VDJ78 Troopie

How It Works

A club built around access, not ownership.

Adventure Club Australia is being developed as a membership-based model where members can build credits, access vehicles and help grow the fleet over time.

STEP 01

Join

Become part of the founding community and help shape the future of the club.

STEP 02

Build Credits

Members build adventure credits that can be used toward future trips and vehicle access.

STEP 03

Book

Choose the vehicle that suits your getaway, from weekend escapes to off-grid expeditions.

STEP 04

Explore

Hit the road, create memories and become part of Australia’s shared adventure fleet.
